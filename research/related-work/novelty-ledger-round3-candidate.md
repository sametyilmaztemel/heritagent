## 1. Current novelty position (2026-10-13, Round 3 completion pass; Round-2 wording of 2026-10-12 retained and extended; supersedes 2026-10-02)

**Intended claim:** HeritAgent's contribution is the *integration and the gating semantics*: an explicit somatic/germline separation for LLM agents, a typed executable functional agent genome (EAG) as the first-class heritable artifact, and a Causal Inheritance Gate (replay → controlled ablation → attribution → held-out generalization → regression protection) as a **prerequisite** for germline assimilation — evaluated across generations with frozen foundation-model weights.

**Round-2 narrowing of the claim (Claude, third agent; pending cross-review):** the claim is retained, but it must now be stated as the *conjunction* only. Each of its parts — pre-persistence gating of learned skills with interventional evidence, an inheritable-artifact vs private-state boundary, probation/promotion tiers for candidate skills, component-typed versioned harnesses, regression-protected admission of heritable variants, and biology-framed multigenerational inheritance of acquired skills on a frozen base — now has published or concurrent prior art in some form (see table, §2.10, §3). What no found work does is to gate the promotion of **individually validated lifetime-acquired traits** from a **somatic staging store** into a **typed genome of policies + skills + regulation** on **trait-level causal evidence**, and to measure that across **agent generations** (held-out performance, regression, genome size, descendant adaptation cost) against both unrestricted and performance-gated inheritance.

**Round-3 completion pass (2026-10-13; Claude, third agent; pending cross-review):** the full reads confirm the Round-2 conjunction wording and change nothing in what is *claimed*; they change what is *conceded* and how the gate must be described. (a) The within-lifetime gate cluster is now read in full: most of its gates are in-sample (SkillBoost, SkillAdam, HarnessEvolve, HarnessX, GSE, Designer-RSI, Self-Healing Harness, SkillCommit) and the held-out ones (GRASP, VaG, SAGE, RSEA, SkillOpt, HDSO's confirmation stage) act on a whole artifact or a single edit. HDSO already splits its validation into in-scope and out-of-scope (guardrail) tasks with staged screening → confirmation, on ALFWorld under the same frozen-Qwen regime as EXP-0001, so "in-scope + out-of-scope held-out probe before persistence" is no longer ours either. (b) Typed-component claims narrow further: HarnessX (runtime-contract-checked, substitutable, hashable configurations), GSE (typed dependency / co-usage / conflict relations between skills), SkillCommit (per-skill validated-scope, version and audit records) and HDSO (typed JSON skill packages with executable hooks and per-skill hypothesis/evidence records) are prior art for each of these features individually; what remains is the *combination* of trait types (policy / skill / regulation) with per-trait validation records as the heritable unit. (c) Statistically certified admission is not ours to claim at all: the Statistical Gödel Machine (arXiv:2510.10232, Oct 2025; abstract page only) and SEA (2026) precede CIG, and SAGE supplies the exact discordant-pair test CIG should borrow. (d) No read work has agent generations with distinct lifetimes: every "generation" in SEA, RSEA, SkillBoost, HarnessX, SkillOpt, HDSO and AHE is a version or rewrite iteration of one artifact, and every cross-model result is a one-hop frozen transfer without re-validation on the recipient. The claim therefore keeps its Round-2 form; the load-bearing words are *trait-level*, *germline assimilation*, *agent generations with distinct lifetimes* and *descendant adaptation cost*.

### 2.1 AgentSquare — ICLR 2025 (venue to confirm)

- **Paper:** *AgentSquare: Automatic LLM Agent Search in Modular Design Space* — Yu Shang, Yu Li, Keyu Zhao, Likai Ma, Jiahe Liu, Fengli Xu, Yong Li (Tsinghua). arXiv:2410.06153 (Oct 2024, v3 Feb 2025). Code: github.com/tsinghua-fib-lab/AgentSquare. *[R2 citation check: equal-contribution asterisks removed — the arXiv page shows none; "ICLR 2025" is not stated on the arXiv page and must be confirmed via OpenReview/ICLR proceedings before citing the venue (SPEC §3 also states ICLR 2025).]*
- **Core mechanism:** MoLAS modular design space — agent = (Planning, Reasoning, Tool Use, Memory) with uniform IO interfaces; LLM-driven module evolution + recombination search with an in-context performance surrogate.
- **Overlap:** typed modular agent spec (genome-like but flat 4-tuple); module pools retained by whole-agent fitness; frozen weights; iterative/generational search. Verified: +17.2% avg over best human design across 6 benchmarks (matches SPEC §3).
- **What remains different:** no trajectory-mined lifetime capabilities; no somatic/germline separation; no per-trait causal gate (surrogate scores ≠ controlled ablation); no versioned genome with skills/regulation; search is per-task and one-time, not cross-lifetime inheritance.
- **Required architecture changes:** none. Cite as modular-search prior art; position EAG's versioning/skills/regulation against their flat tuple.

### 2.2 Darwin Gödel Machine (DGM) — 2025 preprint

- **Paper:** *Darwin Gödel Machine: Open-Ended Evolution of Self-Improving Agents* — Jenny Zhang, Shengran Hu, Cong Lu, Robert Lange, Jeff Clune (UBC/Vector/Sakana). arXiv:2505.22954 (May 2025). *[R2 citation check: verified — title, authors, date, 20.0→50.0% SWE-bench and 14.2→30.7% Polyglot match the abstract.]*
- **Core mechanism:** self-modifying coding agent (whole Python codebase = genotype); archive-based branching tree; parent selection ∝ performance + progeny viability; compile + editing-functionality regression check + benchmark subset as the only gate.
- **Overlap:** generations, lineages, archive, frozen weights (element 6 fully); empirical validation of whole agents; stepstones/backtracking.
- **What remains different:** no somatic store (traits go straight into inherited code); no per-trait credit assignment; gate is coarse (compile + 10-task check + 50-task benchmark) and was shown gameable (reward hacking documented); genome is untyped arbitrary code; transfer measured post-hoc, not as a gate.
- **Required architecture changes:** none. Cite for archive/lineage design and for the reward-hacking cautionary tale → motivates CIG's multi-stage gate (SPEC §9). Round 2 adds the direct successors Hyperagents (2603.19461) and HGM (2510.21614), §2.11.

### 2.3 Mendel Gödel Machine (MGM) — Aug 2026 preprint

- **Paper:** *Mendel Gödel Machine: Recursive Self-Improving Coding Agents via Comparative Evolution* — Changzhi Liu, Yilun Liu, Sikuan Yan, Volker Tresp, Yunpu Ma (UESTC + LMU Munich/MCML). arXiv:2608.07645. Not a Clune-lab work. *[R2 citation check: title, authors, 7 Aug 2026 and the terms controlled inheritance / reaction-norm mutation / cross-lineage hybridization verified from the abstract; the abstract contains no numbers, so the figures below are body-level and were not re-verified in Round 2.]*
- **Core mechanism:** reaction-norm mutation (edit conditioned on multiple tasks' trajectories to separate genotype-level defects from task-specific accidents) + cross-lineage hybridization (failing agent edited using another lineage's trajectory on the same task). Thompson sampling over Beta posteriors on an archive tree.
- **Overlap:** cross-lineage evidence use, comparative controlled *diagnosis*, executable versioned genotype (untyped code), frozen backbone (verified by cross-model transfer: Polyglot scaffold on DeepSeek 96.89% — body-level figure).
- **What remains different:** their "controlled inheritance" = controlled **diagnosis of defects**, not controlled **gating of heritability** — children enter the archive on a syntactic validity check; usefulness emerges only statistically, post hoc, population-level. No somatic/germline split; no per-trait ablation/attribution/held-out gate; no assimilation step.
- **Required architecture changes:** none to the claim; borrow the reaction-norm insight for CIG replay design (validate a candidate across **multiple** environments, not just its source task — already implied by SPEC §9 replay + generalization stages). Same group's MetaSkill-Evolve (2607.05297) is a second adjacent citation (§2.11).

### 2.4 Genomebook — Mar 2026 preprint

- **Paper:** *Genomebook: Mendelian inheritance of behavioural traits in large language model agents across eight generations* — Manuel Corpas (Univ. of Westminster), bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.03.22.713494 (posted 2026-03-24). No arXiv version. *[R2 citation check: the DOI resolves to a bioRxiv lookup, but bioRxiv returned HTTP 429 twice and web search found no trace; title, author, date and the numbers (26 traits, 60 loci, 8 generations, 626 agents) are carried from Round 1 and are UNVERIFIED in Round 2 — re-check before citing.]*
- **Core mechanism:** 26 behavioural/personality traits → 60 diploid loci (22 autosomes + sex chromosomes), additive/dominant/recessive modes, effect sizes 0.15–0.50; phenotype = SOUL.md + DNA.md prompt injection into frozen Claude; pairwise breeding with compatibility-based selection; 8 generations, 626 agents on a simulated social network.
- **Overlap:** explicit genotype/phenotype vocabulary for LLM agents, Mendelian machinery, multi-generation inheritance, frozen weights (element 6 + genetics framing).
- **What remains different — and this is the key contrast for our paper:** Genomebook's genotype parameterizes **behavioural descriptors** ("leadership drive", "obsessive focus"), not **executable functional capabilities**. No lifetime learning at all (traits hand-authored at founding; heritability is by design, not earned); no task trajectories; no validation gate; authors themselves concede results "may reflect prompt conditioning rather than genetic causation"; no external benchmark.
- **Required architecture changes:** none. This is the cleanest foil for "functional vs behavioural genome" — cite early in Related Work. Round 2 adds a second biology-framed foil with *acquired* skills and a frozen base: Gilestro 2026 (§2.10.5).

### 2.5 SkillRL — Feb 2026 (NeurIPS 2026 per arXiv comment)

- **Paper:** *SkillRL: Evolving Agents via Recursive Skill-Augmented Reinforcement Learning* — Peng Xia et al. (13 authors; Huaxiu Yao last; aiming-lab). arXiv:2602.08234 (v1 9 Feb 2026). **Status update 2026-10-04: accepted at NeurIPS 2026.** *[R2 citation check: the only evidence is the arXiv Comments field "NeurIPS 2026"; acceptance was not independently verified — write "NeurIPS 2026 (per arXiv comment)" until the proceedings listing is checked.]* Trait Miner v0 (#9) explicitly credits SkillRL's experience-based differential skill distillation (success/failure trajectories → reusable skills) as the adapted acquisition mechanism; HeritAgent's distinction remains the somatic→germline lifecycle with causal gating.
- **Core mechanism:** o3-teacher differential distillation from success/failure trajectories → SkillBank (general + task-specific skills with `when_to_apply`), GRPO **weight training** (weights NOT frozen), recursive skill-library growth at validation checkpoints (≤3 skills/update, triggered by weak categories <0.4 success).
- **Overlap:** trajectory → reusable skill extraction with activation conditions (element 1 + primitive regulation). *[R2 citation check — numbers corrected:]* ALFWorld 89.9% success; WebShop **72.7% success** (the 85.2 figure is the WebShop *score* in Table 1, not success); Search-QA average 47.1%; base model Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, teacher o3. These are body-level figures; the abstract states only ">15.3%".

- **Required architecture changes:** adopt their success/failure differential distillation as Trait Miner v0 (cite — adapted, not claimed); their SkillBank→Top-K is exactly the "unrestricted/persistent-skill baseline" arm B of EXP-0001. *[R2: SAPO 2606.08755 seeds its bank from SkillRL's and adds a paired with/without pre-storage gate — see §2.10.6 and §5.]*

### 2.6 ShapleyFlow — AISTATS 2021

#### 2.10.14 SAGE — A Statistical Acceptance Gate for Self-Evolving Agents — Sep 2026 preprint — threat LOW (full read, Round 3; lowered from MEDIUM; relevance to EXP-0001 statistics UP)

- **Paper:** *SAGE: A Statistical Acceptance Gate for Self-Evolving Agents* — Yihao Wang, Linhan Xia, Rui Liu, Zhaofeng Zhang, Hongyu Wu, Yang Yang, Jinglu He, Yu Guo, Kai Lei. arXiv:2609.36043 (cs.AI, 28 Sep 2026). Note: `related-work/round3/sage-statistical-acceptance-gate.md`.
- **Core mechanism:** an acceptance gate added to SkillOpt (2605.23904) with the edit proposer held fixed; frozen model; one persistent natural-language skill document. Baseline gate g0 accepts an edit if the aggregate validation score strictly improves. SAGE: run incumbent S and candidate S + e on identical held-out items; count wins w and regressions l; discounted gain Δ = w − λl (λ ≥ 1, citing asymmetric counterfactual utilities) and regression fraction ρ = l / n_S (n_S = items the incumbent already solves); one-sided exact conditional binomial on the discordant pairs p = Pr[Bin(m, λ/(1+λ)) ≥ w], m = w + l (exact McNemar at λ = 1). Accept iff Δ > 0 ∧ p < α ∧ ρ ≤ τ, otherwise abstain; m = 0 always abstains; (λ, α, τ) = (1, 1, 1) recovers g0. Paper-stated limits: binary verifier only; per-comparison error control; the validation set is reused across steps, so trajectory-level error is uncontrolled.
- **Evidence:** 5 text benchmarks × 4 open backbones under an equal budget: regression rate falls in 19/20 cells (0.0% in all 12 LiveMath, SSB and OfficeQA cells); highest final score in 20/20; mean SkillOpt → C1 (paired criterion, no test) +6.20, C1 → SAGE +2.53, total +8.73 (recomputed from Table 2; matches). Weak points: point estimates only — no seeds, CIs, set sizes, hyperparameter values or code; the regression-rate source (validation or test) is unstated, so the 0% may hold by construction; the "boundary property verified in the ablation" has no matching table; how several accepted edits in one mini-batch are combined is unspecified.
- **Chain coverage (full read):** 1 F (only through SkillOpt) · 2 – · 3 – · 4 P · 5 P · 6 P (unchanged).
- **Overlap:** the most statistically careful single-stage gate in the cluster; an explicit argument that paired discordant-pair tests beat shrinking aggregate scores under item correlation; a fixed-optimizer, gate-only ablation (+6.2 then +2.5) showing the acceptance rule alone moves final performance — exactly the confound the performance-gated arm must control.
- **What remains different:** one agent, one untyped live document, edit-level single-stage in-distribution acceptance into the next incumbent; never a prerequisite for heritability; no trait types, regulation layer, per-trait validation records, assimilation event between stores, agent generations, descendants or lineage; 'causal', 'inherit', 'lineage', 'population' absent from the text. CIG's remaining claim is the conjunction plus the cross-generational comparison; SAGE sharpens the performance-gated comparator but does not occupy it.
- **Required architecture changes:** none. **Requires ADR:** (1) performance-gate option — SAGE-style: admit a trait iff Δ_λ > 0, p_λ < α, ρ ≤ τ on a shared held-out set at the parent-genome incumbent, pre-registered λ = 1, α = 0.05 and τ, with no replay/ablation/attribution/out-of-scope stages; the strongest published single-stage performance gate, so not a strawman (§5.1); (2) exact paired discordant-pair test for CIG stages 2–4 in place of, or beside, the aggregate bootstrap CI LB; stage-3 in-scope Δ ≥ 0 → abstain at m = 0 or a pre-registered non-inferiority margin (§5.4); (3) regression denominator — check which denominator EXP-0001's τ_r uses against l / n_S and GRASP's R(c) ≤ R0 so rates are comparable (§5.4); (4) multiplicity across candidates, stages and generations — Holm or BH across a generation's candidates, fresh held-out items per generation, or SEA-style anytime-valid control (§5.4); (5) power — at λ = 1, α = 0.05 at least 5 discordant pairs are needed even if all are wins (0.5⁵ = 0.031); with 20–40 episodes per candidate most candidates will abstain, so pre-register an MDE/discordance calculation and report rejected-on-evidence separately from abstained-for-lack-of-evidence, otherwise abstention confounds the H5 genome-size claims (§5.4, §5.18); (6) several seeds per item break pair independence — cluster by item or stratify (§5.4). Reporting (w, l, m) per trait per stage needs no ADR. Never use "SAGE" for a HeritAgent component; §3 items 11–12 already concede paired, regression-aware and abstaining acceptance.

#### 2.10.21 Designer-RSI — Evolving Procedural Memory from User Traffic — Sep 2026 preprint — threat MEDIUM (full read, Round 3; new entry; Round 2 had only a search snippet)

- **Paper:** *Designer-RSI: Evolving Procedural Memory from User Traffic for Agentic Graphic Design* — Du, Yan, Flores, Kadav (Adobe / Brown). arXiv:2609.22086. - **Overlap:** a per-change paired intervention with explicit confound control (frozen per-context upstream state, same-batch replay, order-swapped pairwise judging), with mints tested with/without the single trait — roughly CIG stages 1–2 plus local regression protection; a typed, versioned SKILL.md schema with a status field. Citation only (no ADR): its round-4 regression from mints gated only against the no-skill agent motivates CIG attribution against the current genome — a single-run observation. 12. **Placebo traits for gate calibration (new).** Add one or two length-matched irrelevant traits to arm C's frozen candidate set and report their per-stage pass rate as the gate's false-pass rate (≈ 130 episodes per placebo per seed). Needed to interpret H2 and H5 and the rejected-vs-abstained split in item 4(c). **Requires ADR.**
13. **No in-sample evidence (new).** State that the episodes a trait was mined from are not reused as stage-2/3 evidence (stage-1 source replay is the labelled exception) and which stages, if any, are allowed in-sample; stage-1 replay must be paired with/without on the same seeds or k ≥ 2 samples per arm, never using the observed failure as the baseline (SkillCommit's single-sample failure-selected admission and regression to the mean; SkillAdam Fig. 4; HarnessEvolve's training-batch gate; SkillBoost's training-split back-test). **Requires ADR if not already specified.**
14. **Leakage / bloat hygiene filter identical across arms (new).** Reject traits that embed training queries or answers (string match + LLM judge) and cap injected examples per trait, applied to A / B / performance-gated / C alike so arm B cannot gain from memorised answers (HarnessEvolve's quality gate, the one gate it ablates: −6.8); ties to τ_k. **Requires ADR if it becomes part of the CIG; pre-register either way.**
15. **Delivery-interface confound (new).** HDSO's Table 3 suggests full-context delivery shrinks gains relative to progressive disclosure (+1.6 vs +6.9 for the same 8B repository — my inference), so a C > B result could be read as a prompt-load effect: keep delivery identical across B, the performance-gated arm and C; optional secondary arm — B with retrieval / progressive-disclosure delivery (in addition to item 5's token-matched control). **Requires ADR to add an arm.**
16. **Composite candidates (new).** Forbid co-shipping several candidate traits into one evaluation unit unless the design is factorial or leave-one-out; split or per-component ablate bundled candidates in stage 2 before assimilation (AHE; HarnessX). Sub-additive, overlapping traits can make leave-one-out stage-2 ablation reject all redundant traits (RSEA Table 5): consider group ablation or ablation against the empty genome. **Requires ADR.**
17. **Expression precondition and expression-conditioned regression (new).** No credit on episodes where the trait never fired (HarnessX attribution_signature; HDSO uptake log; SPEC stage-2 expression check) — citation only; ADR to make it a hard gate condition. Also report stage-4 regression on the T_reg subset where the candidate actually fired (GSE), since narrow traits dilute τ_r on the full 40-task suite — secondary analysis; ADR only if it replaces T_reg.
18. **CIG record / EAG trait schema additions (new; ADR-0001 amendment).** falsification_plan, rollback_condition, uptake count and a reject-reason taxonomy separating harm from under-powering (HDSO); an abstain verdict with δ-spent fields (SEA); a declared applicability scope plus exclusion condition (SkillCommit W(p) / avoid_when), with the out-of-scope probe testing the declared exclusion; *measured* pairwise interaction records (GSE's SRG — phrase as "measured interaction records", not new typed relations; the v0 "harmful pair → keep higher-contribution trait" rule discards this); pre-registered predicted unlock / at-risk sets with hit rate incl. collateral damage (HarnessX; AHE); a possible "split / narrow-scope assimilation" third outcome, counted separately and re-validated on fresh items (SkillCommit); CIG rejections (score drop, failing stage) stored as lineage-graph evidence (SkillOpt's buffer ablation).
19. **Optimizer / somatic memory as an uncontrolled inheritance channel (new).** Pre-register whether descendants can read ancestors' rejected-trait records, issue histories or CIG rejection evidence (SkillAdam's issue tracker; SkillOpt's rejected buffer and never-shipped meta skill; HDSO's hypothesis ledger fed back as negative evidence); if they can, it is inheritance outside the EAG and must be constant across arms, which must also share the same within-lifetime learner; in multi-generation runs it must be symmetric across arms or ablated. **Requires ADR (ADR-0001 / 0002 clarification).**
20. **Judge separation (new).** If EXP-0001 uses an LLM judge anywhere, keep the CIG judge and the held-out evaluation judge distinct or report both (Designer-RSI deliberately uses one judge family for the evolution reward and the reported metric). **Requires ADR if a judge is used.**
21. **Reporting (no ADR).** Overfitting gap Δ = held-out − replay score per arm and generation (SkillBoost Table III); val-to-test gap per generation (RSEA); final-generation and held-out numbers, never peak-over-rounds, with selection and evaluation sets separate (HarnessX; AHE; DarwinX); a multi-seed noise floor on identical genomes (SEA's two control-configuration runs 29 vs 36 / 52; SkillEvoReg's ≈ 9 pp divergence) — supports 3 mining seeds and the "C−B direction in ≥ 2/3 seeds" rule and warns against reading single-seed 5–10 pp differences; the per-episode paired-delta distribution (variance, n10/n01) beside mean Δ (SkillAdam); candidates evaluated per accepted trait (SkillOpt); gate cost and descendant adaptation cost in optimization-phase tokens and API requests (SkillAdam); per-arm expression tokens by execution quartile (Self-Healing Harness); regression denominator and per-family breakdowns; "non-inferior to arm A on held-out" as an observed property, not an assumed one (SPEC §34); re-run any SkillOpt / SkillAdam comparators rather than copying their numbers; always report the default configuration.
