## Why this ADR: ADR-0011 D6's trigger
- **The trigger.** ADR-0011 D6 (owner, 2026-10-16, "Bana sor"): if the Qwen3.8-27B pilot measures G0 above 60 % at temperature 0, or its temperature-0 repeats differ, builder-2 reports and the owner decides how to go on.
- **Both fired (R, pilot report).** G0 solved 20/30 games at temperature 0 (66.7 %, Wilson [0.49, 0.81]). The six temperature-0 repeats all had different trajectories; their outcomes were equal.
- **The owner's decisions.** builder-2 reported both; the owner asked for the analysis, which showed that the locked gate almost never promotes a realistic helpful trait at these rates (Problem). The owner then decided to recalibrate the gate ("Geçidi düzelt", 2026-10-16) and chose the pooled package of three ("POOLED (Önerilen)", 2026-10-17). This ADR states that package; the owner accepted each decision on 2026-10-19 (The owner's answers).
- **Unchanged.** Qwen3.8-27B stays the model (ADR-0011 D1), with its serving, prompts and sampling.
- **This ADR addresses** the gate's false rejection of helpful candidates at a high G0. **It does not resolve:**
  - *Headroom.* At G0 near 0.67, an arm can gain at most about +33 pp on T_eval at δ = 0, and about +31 pp at δ = 0.05 and +23 pp at δ = 0.18 for a uniform observed G0 of 0.667 (+25 pp at the pilot's per-family rates), since even a perfect trait is observed at 1 − ν (M). pick_and_place, solved 5/5, shows no measured headroom, and each family's rate rests on five games. H1 and H2 keep their rules (ADR-0006), so their power at a given arm-level effect is unchanged. H2 also needs arm B to carry candidates that harm (Research implication), which no pilot number informs (U), and even then its joint power stays modest (0.11 at +5 pp and 0.53 at +10 pp, δ = 0.05; Evidence).
  - *Backend noise.* The server still has no batch-invariant kernels (ADR-0011 D2). This ADR keeps every comparison inside one round's queue (D2), states every figure at δ = 0, 0.05 and 0.18 and adds descriptive measures (D8); it does not reduce the noise.
  - *The design's G0 expectation* (about 30 %, ADR-0007 E1, ADR-0009) stays as recorded, with a dated note (D11).

## Problem
- **The locked gate rejects helpful candidates at the pilot's G0 (M).** At the pilot's per-family temperature-0 rates, a candidate with a true +10 pp gain in every family (nominal, capped by each family's headroom; task model) is promoted with probability 0.006–0.014, and one with +15 pp with 0.030–0.042, for backend disagreement δ = 0 to 0.18. The rates are pick_and_place 5/5, two_obj 4/5, look, clean and heat 3/5, cool 2/5 (R). A +25 pp candidate scoped to two families is promoted with 0.011–0.016.
- **Why.** Three small outcome checks must all pass. Replay (5 games) needs successes_with ≥ 3 and net ≥ +2, hence at least two G0 failures among five games, so even a perfect trait passes only about 0.5–0.64 of the time. Ablation uses one 20-game pool whatever the scope and, with no break, needs at least 4 net rescues against about 6 G0 failures in the pool (V for the count table, M for the expectation), diluting a narrow trait's rescues. The in-scope check uses 10 games.
- **Consequence (M).** With 10 candidates per seed (3 at +15 pp all-family, 2 at +25 pp two-family, 5 with no effect), arm C is empty in a seed with probability 0.85–0.89, and in all three seeds with 0.65–0.72. An empty C is G0 (ADR-0009 D1), so C − A is exactly 0 and H1 cannot be supported.
- **Promoting no-effect traits is not the problem.** Under the locked rules a no-effect trait is promoted with probability at most 4.4e-4 (δ = 0.18). The failure is false rejection.
- **Backend noise.** Six repeats bound identical-prompt disagreement only below about 0.39 (M); changed-prompt disagreement is unmeasured (U); the pilot's temperature-0.7 attempt-pair disagreement, 16/90 = 0.178 (R), is the upper planning anchor.

## Current design
- **Stage 1:** source task plus 4 `replay_bank[family]` games, with and without the trait. Pass iff successes_with ≥ 3/5, net ≥ +2/5 and the expression check.
- **Stage 2:** the 20-game ablation pool. Pass iff the point estimate ≥ τ_c = 0.05 and the ADR-0005 bootstrap lower bound > 0.
- **Stage 3:** in-scope, 10 `generalization_bank` games, pass iff Δ ≥ 0; out-of-scope, 10 games, pass iff Δ ≥ −0.05 (no net loss at n = 10); vacuous for an all-family scope (ADR-0007 E6).
- **Stage 4:** 4A T_reg, 40 tasks, paired against one G0 run per seed, pass iff net loss ≤ 1 (ADR-0009 D1); 4B overhead ≤ 512 tokens; 4C both vs neither on 10 games, harmful iff Δ_pair < −0.05.
- **Execution:** every stage runs its own without-trait executions, except T_reg's shared G0 run; the gate runs in three parallel stretches (ADR-0008 D3).
- **Caps:** 7,000 episodes, 225,000 calls, 96 active hours.

## Decisions (accepted by the owner, 2026-10-19)

### D1 A benefit bank in its own manifest
- **Rule text.**
  - Pool `benefit_bank` (ALFWorld 0.2.2 train): per family, the 192 loader-accepted train games that are in no pool of `EXP-0001-splits.json` and not among the 30 games of `EXP-0001-pilot.json`, taken by the smallest `sha256("heritagent-split|0|benefit_bank|<family>|<game_id>")` (the split builder's keyed-hash rule); 1,152 games. Within a family the bank order is ascending by that hash.
  - It is recorded in a new manifest, `experiments/manifests/EXP-0001-benefit-bank.json`, with its own sha256 and the split (`2f74d188…ed9c`) and pilot (`4fc2b6fe…aa52`) manifest hashes it was drawn against; building it makes no model call.
  - A family with fewer than 192 such games fails closed. ADR-0004's fallback is a split-level rule (ADR-0009 D5) and does not cover this bank; ADR-0004 is not edited.
- **Availability (V, manifest counts re-read by two evaluations).** Games left per family: look 277, pick_and_place 754, clean 615, cool 499, heat 427, two_obj 777; the smallest margin is 85.
- **Unchanged.** `EXP-0001-splits.json`, the split plan, the pilot manifest and both hashes. `replay_bank` and `generalization_bank` stay in the split manifest, unused by the gate; the ablation pool stays the transfer subset (ADR-0007 E4).

### D2 Round-scoped G0 reference cells, per seed
- **Rounds.** A seed's gate runs in six rounds: R1 stage 1 and stage-2 look 1; R2–R4 stage-2 looks 2–4 for every candidate still running; R5 stage 3 (out of scope) and stage 4A; R6 stage 4C. A round starts after the previous round's episodes are recorded, because they decide who continues.
- **Cells.** In each round, G0 (no trait) plays once, at the gate's locked settings (temperature 0, seed = the mining seed, the ReAct stop, the request budget), every game that some with-trait execution of that round is compared with. That execution is the game's cell for (seed, round). Every without-trait outcome of the round (stages 2, 3 and 4A) and every neither outcome (4C) reads it.
- **A cell is read only in its round.** A later round that needs the same game plays it again. So a game can have up to five G0 executions per seed (R1–R5); none is pooled with another or read outside its round.
- **One queue per round.** The round's cells and with-trait executions form one queue in ascending sha256 of their ledger keys and run as one dependency-free stretch (amends ADR-0008 D3's three stretches). Every comparison's two executions are therefore in the same stretch, and neither condition is systematically earlier, later or in another session.
- **Lazy and recorded.** A cell is played only in a round that needs it, so the gate records no episode it did not request (the unrequested-episode check stays). Each child names the cells it read (seed, round, game); reuse within a round is logged (design §4). Cells are never shared across seeds or with the final evaluation.
- **T_reg and the interaction pool** are read only in R5 and R6, so their cells are ADR-0009 D1's single G0 run per seed and task.
- **Extends ADR-0009 D1.** "One execution per (task instance, condition)" becomes one per (seed, round, task, condition), shared by that round's candidates.
- **Why rounds (M).** Under revision 0, a cell played in the first round that needed it was read by every later round: in the mixture 18–29 % of stage-2 with-trait episodes read an earlier round's cell, while the α budget tolerates only about 1 pp of systematic offset (an offset on every comparison of 1 pp gives 0.09 and of 2 pp 0.43 expected false promotions over 30 no-effect candidates at δ = 0.05: revision 0's verification, reproduced by builder-2 and by revision 2's check; on stage 2 alone, 0.09 and 0.41: revision 1's re-run).
- **Cost (M, matched runs).** Round-scoped cells add 244–449 episodes on average in the mixture over revision 0 (about 970–980 in the worst replicate seen; 305–562 at δ = 0.18 over the no-effect scopes, task model); none when every scope is all-family, about 70 when every scope is one family, and 709–894 (up to 1,446) when helpful candidates have mixed narrow scopes. Side effect: a seed's stage-3 comparisons no longer share noise with its stage-2 comparisons, which lowers the per-seed emptiness at δ = 0.18 from 0.064 to 0.049 at equal per-candidate promotion (M).

### D3 Stage 1 becomes an expression check on the source task
- **Rule text.** The candidate plays its source task once with the trait at the gate's settings; the source task is the T_mine game of its mining pair's success (ADR-0007 E6). It passes iff the target gene is expressed in that episode or the episode fails: the locked expression check applied to one episode. There is no outcome threshold and no without-trait execution.
- **What it tests.** That a successful replay of the trait expresses its gene. It does **not** test that the trait reproduces its outcome where it was found: a failed episode passes, and nothing is compared. Its pass probability is assumed to be 0.95 (U).
- Outcome evidence moves to stage 2, on unseen in-scope games, not on the source task. Outcome replays are Q6's alternatives.

### D4 Stage 2 becomes a pooled, group-sequential exact paired sign test
- **Allocation.** 192 `benefit_bank` games over the candidate's frozen applicability families (m families, in name order): allocation game j (0-based) is the ⌊j/m⌋-th game, in bank order, of the (j mod m)-th family. Every candidate that needs n games of a family gets the same first n; reuse is logged, with no replacement within a candidate.
- **Looks** after n₁…n₄ = 48, 96, 144 and 192 games. At look k the candidate plays games [n₍k−1₎, n_k) once each with the trait, in round R_k, against that round's cells (D2).
- **Counts over the first n_k games.** n⁺ = games the with-trait execution solved and the cell failed; n⁻ = the reverse; net = n⁺ − n⁻; point = net / n_k; p_k = P(Bin(n⁺ + n⁻, ½) ≥ n⁺), computed with integers and `Fraction`; p_k = 1 if n⁺ + n⁻ = 0.
- **Efficacy.** Pass at look k iff p_k ≤ α_k and point ≥ τ_c = 0.05, with (α₁, α₂, α₃, α₄) = (0.0005, 0.0005, 0.001, 0.003).
- **Futility.** At looks 1–3, stop and fail iff net ≤ ⌊n_k/40⌋ (1, 2 and 3). Without efficacy at look 4, the stage fails.
- **Records.** Games are never filtered by expression; expression is recorded. The child keeps every paired outcome in allocation order with its task id and cell, the per-look counts, each p_k, the stopping look and reason, and the ADR-0005 percentile bootstrap interval over the games played (seed 0, task_id order, 10,000 resamples), descriptive only. The candidate's stage-2 contribution is its point at the stopping look.
- **The gate recomputes the decision from the record** (as ADR-0009 D5 does for the bootstrap) before any R5 episode and whenever a run reads its gate artifact back.
- **False-promotion budget.**
  - A no-effect candidate (on every game, P(with-trait solves, cell fails) = P(the reverse)) passes stage 2 with probability at most α₁ + … + α₄ = 0.005, at any backend noise: each look is an exact conditional sign test of size ≤ α_k, a union bound gives the sum, and futility only lowers it.
  - Per seed (K ≤ 10), the probability that any no-effect candidate passes stage 2 is at most 0.05 (Bonferroni, valid under any dependence, D2's shared cells included); over 30 candidates the expected number is at most 0.15.
  - The bound needs a game's with-trait execution and its cell to be exchangeable for a no-effect trait (U). D2 removes the identified systematic source (time and session); Q9 offered a direct check, which the owner declined.
  - The largest null size over every discordance rate is 0.0032 (M, exact DP).
- **Below τ_c.** τ_c is a rule on the point estimate, not a bound on the true effect. A trait whose true gain is 3–4 pp on every game is promoted with 0.05–0.19 at δ = 0, 0.04–0.10 at δ = 0.05 and 0.02–0.03 at δ = 0.18 (M, pilot rates).
- **Tables checked (V).** The decision tables match a direct `Fraction` evaluation of this rule text in all 35,284 (n⁺, n⁻) cells.

### D5 Stage 3: the in-scope child records stage 2; out-of-scope uses 24 games at the locked tolerance
- **In-scope.** The `generalization_in_scope` child records stage 2's paired outcomes by reference and passes iff Δ ≥ 0 over the games stage 2 played (the locked in-scope rule). It runs no episode.
  - **It adds no test.** At every look, p_k ≤ α_k implies net > 0, and at looks 1–3 it also implies point ≥ τ_c (V, every (n⁺, n⁻) cell enumerated). So whenever stage 3 runs (D7), the child passes, and it can change no verdict.
  - It is kept because the record store requires the five M0 stage keys (D7) and because stage 2's games are the held-out in-scope evidence SPEC §9 asks stage 3 to provide.
- **Out-of-scope**, when the frozen applicability omits at least one family:
  - 24 `benefit_bank` games over the omitted families, round-robin in name order, each family's first games in bank order (24, 12, 8, 6, or 5/5/5/5/4 games per omitted family), played in R5 against R5's cells. They are disjoint from the candidate's stage-2 games.
  - Pass iff Δ_out ≥ −0.05 in exact arithmetic, i.e. net loss (cell successes minus with-trait successes) ≤ 1 of 24. The threshold is unchanged; only n changes.
- **All-family applicability:** vacuous pass (ADR-0007 E6, unchanged).

### D6 Stage 4
- **4A, unchanged rule:** T_reg in R5, paired against the seed's T_reg cells, pass iff net loss ≤ 1 (τ_r = 0.03).
- **4B, unchanged:** at most 512 tokens.
- **4C:** pool, contrast and threshold unchanged (both vs neither on 10 games, harmful iff Δ_pair < −0.05, ADR-0002 A1). Neither reads the seed's R6 cells, so a pair costs 10 both-trait episodes. A harmful pair removes the member with the lower stage-2 contribution; on an exact tie the earlier frozen rank is kept.

### D7 Which stages run, the record keys, and threshold sensitivity
- **Which stages run.** Stages 1, 2 and 4B run for every candidate, and the in-scope child is computed for every candidate from its stage-2 record. Stage 3's out-of-scope check and 4A run for every candidate whose stage-2 p_k met α_k at some look, whatever its point estimate; only (n⁺, n⁻) = (9, 0) at look 4 meets α without the point rule (V). For the others, the out-of-scope child and the 4A and 4C parts of the interaction_regression child carry `stage_pass: false`, `measurements.not_run: true` and the reason; no τ_c in {0.03, 0.05, 0.08} or τ_r in {0.02, 0.03, 0.05} could change their verdicts.
- **Record keys (V, `genome/validation/records.py`).** An M0 bundle needs exactly one child for each of five keys, kept with their names: `replay` (D3), `ablation` (D4), `generalization_in_scope` (D5, by reference), `generalization_out_of_scope` (D5) and `interaction_regression` (4A, 4B, 4C). All five are written for every candidate, each with ADR-0010 B1's binding fields, so the store's checks hold unchanged.
- **Order within a seed:** D2's rounds R1–R6.
- **Threshold sensitivity (secondary).** The τ_c grid is recomputed on the recorded stage-2 paths; a cell in which another τ_c would have continued past the observed stopping look is recorded as not identifiable. The τ_r grid is recomputed on 4A where it ran. τ_c = 0.05 rarely binds: at most 1.8e-4 of a no-effect candidate's mass meets α_k but fails the point rule (M); only 0.08 can bind.

### D8 Repeats, the temperature-0.7 attempts and descriptive noise measures
- No gate decision uses repeated executions or the lifetime's temperature-0.7 attempts.
- The report gives, descriptively, with exact 95 % intervals:
  - the outcome disagreement between a seed's cells of one game in two rounds: identical requests (same prompt, request seed and settings) in different batches, about 80–160 pairs per seed in the mixture (M);
  - the disagreement between seeds' cells of one game (same prompt; the request seeds differ, which greedy decoding should not use, U).
- They decide nothing and cost no episode. They are lower references for the changed-prompt disagreement a with/without contrast carries, not estimates of it. They replace the 48-episode diagnostic the pooled package proposed.

### D9 Compute caps and a final-evaluation reserve
- **Caps:** episodes_max 14,500; model_calls_max 507,500 (35 per episode, so that the call cap binds first only above 35 calls per episode); wall_clock_hours_max 96, active run time (unchanged, ADR-0008 D2); accelerator_nodes_max 1; mining seeds [11, 23, 41]; the stop-mark-incomplete rule unchanged.
- **Final-evaluation reserve (Q10: yes).** Before each gate round, the cap check requires room for the round's largest possible count given the candidates still in it (at most 960, V arithmetic) plus the final evaluation's largest (1,358); otherwise the run stops before the round, marked incomplete. The same look-ahead applies to the call cap, with those episode counts converted at the run's mean calls per episode so far (recorded in the check). The per-episode worst-case check (ADR-0007 E5) stays. An episode-cap stop then never falls inside the final evaluation, where it would leave no primary outcome (ADR-0006 D5) after spending up to 1,358 episodes. A call-cap stop can, chiefly when the final evaluation's episodes average more calls than the run's earlier ones. Active hours are not looked ahead: at 345 episodes per hour (R) the episode cap takes about 42 of the 96 allowed.
- **Basis.**
  - The 7,000 cap is exceeded in the mixture with probability up to 0.77 (pilot rates, δ = 0.18) and in most pessimistic worlds (M).
  - 14,500 is above the largest total seen in 5.55 million simulated experiments over every evaluated world (13,934: ten +10 pp candidates with mixed narrow scopes, G0 0.30, δ = 0.05). No total exceeded 14,000; no cell's estimate of P(> 13,500) exceeded 8e-5 (4 events in 50,000; M). With the reserve's episode look-ahead, no run of eight heavy world cells was stopped at 14,000 or 14,500 (builder-2's run, 8 cells × 40,000; 13,500 stopped 2.5e-5 of one cell; revision 1's re-run, 64,000 runs: none at 14,000 or 14,500, 1 of 8,000 at 13,500). The call look-ahead's stop rate was not simulated; it equals the episode rule's at 35 calls per episode and is tighter above (507,500 / 40 = 12,688 episodes; U).
  - **Arithmetic planning bound (V).** With every candidate running every look and stage, every pair running 4C and every seed's C non-empty, the run needs at most 16,724 episodes (16,454 for a configuration found by search); revision 0's rule gave 14,564. The cap is a resource envelope set from the simulated tails, not from this bound.
  - **Calls.** The locked caps' ratio is 32 per episode (225,000 / 7,000); the pilot's mean is 26.3 (R), and its steps suggest about 35 at a G0 success near 0.30 (M). At 32 per episode (464,000) and 35 calls per episode the call cap would bind first, near 13,257 episodes: in the worst world cell (ten +10 pp candidates with mixed narrow scopes, G0 0.30, δ = 0.05) P(total > 13,257) is 4.8e-4 and P(> 12,000) 0.185 (M). Hence 35 per episode.
  - At 345 episodes per hour (R) the cap takes about 42 active hours (96 allowed) and 315 CU at 7.5 CU/hour.
- This fulfils ADR-0011 D7 (the cap follows the new pilot) and amends D5's "kept as locked" caps.

## Reason
- **Outcome-blind, before execution.** No EXP-0001 episode exists, and the manifest's immutability clause has not started (design §8). The pilot's per-family G0 rates, throughput and calls per episode entered a power and cost model, which motivated the package, chose among its variants and sizes the caps, as ADR-0009 D3 foresaw (the pilot's numbers "inform the compute-cap decision and any later amendment"). The locked τ_c, τ_r and out-of-scope tolerance are kept; α_k, the looks and the sample sizes were set in that model, not on any pilot game's outcome. The α split is not the power-maximising one: splits of the same 0.005 move stage-2 power by up to about 11 pp at δ ≥ 0.05 (equal 0.00125 each: −3 to −8 pp; early-heavy (0.002, 0.001, 0.001, 0.001): −4 to −11 pp; late-heavy (0.0001, 0.0002, 0.0005, 0.0042): +2 to +4 pp, recomputed at 0.0042 and identical to four decimals to a first run at 0.0041; the one alternative evaluated before, (0, 0, 0.0015, 0.0035): +0.6 to +2.0 pp; all over eight cases, pilot rates and G0 0.667 × {+5 pp at δ = 0.05, +10 pp at δ = 0.05 and 0.18, +15 pp at δ = 0.18}), and nothing at δ = 0 (M, exact DP). The recommended split keeps some α at looks 1–2 so that a strong candidate can stop early; that episode saving was not computed (U). Q7 offers the alternatives. Nothing was chosen for its effect on an EXP-0001 outcome. The pilot's 30 games are disjoint from every pool (V) and, by D1, from the bank. This follows the ADR-0009 to ADR-0011 precedent for pre-execution amendments.
- **D4 states a bound on false promotion that holds at any backend noise (M, under exchangeability).** The locked bootstrap gives no stated error rate at n = 20. Promotion of no-effect traits stays small but rises: 3.2e-4 per candidate at δ = 0.05 and 1.4e-3 at δ = 0.18, against the locked gate's 3.2e-6 and 4.4e-4 and MIN-1's 6.4e-4 and 6.4e-3 (M).
- **Power in the model.** Under noise model S, stage-2 power depends on the realised (headroom-capped) gain and δ and not on G0's level while G0 leaves headroom (G0 = 0.667 and 0.30 give the same stage-2 numbers); this is a property of the model, and per-family headroom still limits it (pick_and_place). The replay's need for two G0 failures goes, and every candidate gets 192 games in the families it claims.
- **D2.** Sharing a seed's G0 executions across its candidates, as ADR-0009 D1 does for T_reg, makes 192 games per candidate affordable (without it, up to 216 more without-trait games per candidate and seed). Per-seed cells keep ADR-0007 E8 and ADR-0009 D1 and make the seeds' reference executions independent (Q3); per-round cells make the same-queue claim true by construction.
- **D5 keeps the pre-registered −0.05 tolerance.** At n = 24 a 10 pp spillover passes 0.29–0.36 and a 20 pp spillover 0.03–0.11 (locked: 0.35–0.38 and 0.11–0.19), and a trait without harm is falsely rejected less often (0.07–0.23 against 0.18–0.34 at δ = 0.05–0.18; M). The pooled design's −1/12 passed a 10 pp loss 0.52–0.56.
- **D3 and D5's in-scope child** drop checks that, at their sample sizes, mostly measured noise. They keep SPEC §9's four stages as records, not as four independent tests (Research implication).

### Task model (behind every M figure)
- **Noise model S** (default). Each execution reports its noise-free outcome, flipped with probability ν; δ = 2ν(1 − ν) is the outcome disagreement of two executions of one game and prompt. G0's rates and a trait's gain are expected single-execution (observed) success rates (an input rate above 1 − ν is clipped to 1 − ν). The noise-free rate is q = (p − ν)/(1 − 2ν), so a gain g is a noise-free rise of g/(1 − 2ν): +15 pp is 15.8 pp noise-free at δ = 0.05 and 18.75 pp at δ = 0.18 where the family has that headroom. q is clipped to [0, 1], and a family's gain is capped by its headroom: at pilot rates pick_and_place (5/5, observed at 1 − ν) gains nothing, and two_obj gains at most 20 / 17.4 / 10 pp at δ = 0 / 0.05 / 0.18. The same cap binds at G0 0.667 for +25 pp at δ = 0.18 (23.3 pp realised). Every "+X pp" candidate gain in this ADR is nominal, before that cap; at pilot rates "+15 pp, all six" realises an average observed gain of 12.5 / 11.7 pp at δ = 0.05 / 0.18. The "3–4 pp on every game" rows add that amount on every game, pick_and_place's included, uncapped. Arm-level effects (H1, H2) and spillover losses are not family gains.
- **Noise model F** (fragile games; called model R in revision 1). A game is fragile with probability 2δ; every execution of a fragile game is a fair coin.
- **G0's rates.** Benefit-bank and out-of-scope games: the pilot's per-family temperature-0 rates (R). T_reg: G0's temperature-0 success 20/26, the pilot's rate on the 26 games G0 solved at least once at temperature 0.7 (the T_reg rule; R). pick_and_place is held at 1 and the other five families get the pilot rate times 1.188 (look, clean, heat 0.713; cool 0.475; two_obj 0.950), so that the T_mine-weighted mean, weights (9, 11, 10, 10, 9, 11)/60, is 20/26 = 0.769. The T_reg composition is 6/7/7/7/6/7 over look, pick_and_place, clean, cool, heat, two_obj (assumption, a proportional split of 40); over it the expected success is 0.763 (input rates; observed 0.758 / 0.737 at δ = 0.05 / 0.18). At the other G0 levels, T_reg is 20/26 in every family (G0 0.667) and 0.30 (G0 0.30).
- **Helpful traits** gain on T_reg tasks in their scope as on bank games (without it, +15 pp at δ = 0.18 falls from 0.530 to 0.395). 4B always passes; stage 1 passes with 0.95 (U).
- **The mixture's five no-effect candidates:** each independently all-family with probability ½, otherwise 1–5 random families (size uniform). Their scope moves cost. Emptiness and helpful promotion barely move, and the expected no-effect promotions per replicate (of 15) at δ = 0.18 range over about 0.0105–0.0125 (mixture 0.0115). All-family nulls give mean episodes 6,730 / 7,133 and P(> 7,000) 0.23 / 0.62 at δ = 0.05 / 0.18; narrow-only nulls 6,995 / 7,574 and 0.48 / 0.85; round cells then cost 305–562 more than revision 0 at δ = 0.18 (M; episodes: revision 1's re-run; promotion range: revision 2's check, 200,000 replicates; mixture value: builder-2's run).

## Research implication
- **What each stage tests after this ADR.**

  | Stage (record key) | Episodes per candidate | What it tests | What it no longer tests |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 (`replay`) | 1 with-trait | expression in a successful replay of the source task | that the trait reproduces its outcome where it was found |
  | 2 (`ablation`) | ≤ 192 with-trait, against round cells | benefit on unseen in-scope games, with a stated bound on promoting a no-effect trait | — (the gate's only benefit test) |
  | 3 in-scope (`generalization_in_scope`) | none | nothing beyond stage 2: it passes whenever it can matter (V) | in-scope generalization as a separate test on its own games |
  | 3 out-of-scope (`generalization_out_of_scope`) | 24 | harm outside the declared scope | — |
  | 4 (`interaction_regression`) | 40, and 10 per pair | regression on T_reg, overhead, harmful pairs | — (unchanged) |

  SPEC §9 says replay tests that a trait is reproducible where it was found, and generalization that it did not memorise its source. Stage 2's unseen in-scope games now carry the second; nothing carries the first. The four stages remain as names and records; the decisions rest on stage 2, the out-of-scope check and stage 4. The owner accepted this reading of SPEC §9 (Q6, 2026-10-19); SPEC.md is not edited.
- **Who gets promoted.** Helpful candidates of +10 pp or more become likely to be promoted at low noise. At δ = 0.18 a +10 pp all-family candidate is still promoted with only 0.24 (pilot) to 0.39, and +5 pp with 0.04–0.06; true effects of 3–4 pp, below τ_c, are promoted 5–19 % of the time at δ = 0 (M). Under noise, 4A and 4C remove 13–29 % of no-effect traits and 3–11 % of eligible helpful traits in the mixture, up to 26 % when every candidate is helpful and narrow.
- **Harm screening.** The harm checks keep their thresholds, and out-of-scope screening is at least as strict as now for losses of 10 pp or more. But a trait that helps in scope and harms elsewhere is promoted far more often (0.17–0.25 at a 10 pp loss elsewhere), because its stage-2 test now passes; its T_eval effect is unconditional on scope (threat §6.6(c)).
- **Scope gaming.** The declared scope now also selects the stage-2 sample, which enlarges threat §6.6's surface; the mitigations are unchanged (scope frozen at extraction, the out-of-scope check, T_eval).
- **H1 and H2.** Their rules, and their power at a given arm-level effect, are unchanged; what changes is how often arm C is a distinct genome, which mostly affects H1. Promoted helpful traits sit in arm B too, so C − B is positive only if B also carries candidates that harm and C excludes them. In the mixture, where none harms, C − B is near 0 and H2 is not expected to be supported (R, MIN-1 evaluation); the H2 power row holds only for the story in which B's rejected traits cost B what its helpful traits give. The H2 regression clause's floor at n = 40 (ADR-0006 notes, N5) is untouched.
- **Seed dependence.** The three seeds read the same bank games, so the "two of three seeds" clause is weaker replication evidence than three independent gates would give (M).
- No novelty, causal or "better" claim follows. The figures are model projections, not results.
