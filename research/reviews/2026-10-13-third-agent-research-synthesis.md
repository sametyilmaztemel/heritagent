## 1. Executive summary

1. **As currently specified and coded, EXP-0001 is unlikely to produce an informative H2 test, so the critical path to #13 should start with design ADRs and a calibration pilot, not with the run.** Five verified reasons, none a code bug:
   - **No mining pairs (P0).** The Trait Miner accepts only a success and a failure of the *same* task (`traits/miner/inputs.py:3-4`); the runtime hard-codes temperature 0 (`runtime/loop.py:174`); the design runs one G0 episode per T_mine instance per seed (design:85). The specified protocol yields no same-instance pair, so K ≈ 0 candidates. Pairs could arise only by accident (identical task text across instances, or cross-seed pairing, which is identical at temp 0). The pairing policy was deferred to #13 and never pre-registered. (`VERIFICATION` V1, the only finding that stayed P0.)
   - **T_reg is probably infeasible.** It needs ≥ 40 of 60 T_mine tasks solved by G0 (≥ 66.7 %). Published training-free Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct anchors are 29.9 % (ExpRAG, valid_unseen, temp 0) and 45.5 % (SAGA, same split), with a 14.8–31.2 % family on an unstated split. P(≥ 40/60 | p = 0.45) = 0.0006; expected solved 9–28. (`ALFWorld-baselines.md` finding 4.)
   - **8-step episode cap.** `runtime/seed_g0.py:17` sets `max_plan_steps: 8`, enforced by `effective_max_steps = min(...)` (`runtime/loop.py:151`); the planner schema caps the value at 16, so a 50-step protocol (the SAGA / verl-agent anchors) needs a schema change and a new planner gene. The cap is unregistered, not hidden, and identical across arms. (V4.)
   - **Gate operating characteristics make an empty arm C plausible.** Stage 2 at n = 20 is an integer rule: ≥ 4 net rescues with 0 breaks (observed ≥ +20 pp); the τ_c grid {0.03, 0.05, 0.08} changes 0 of 231 possible decisions. Zero-noise false-reject for a genuinely helpful generalist trait is ≈ 99 % at +10 pp and ≈ 85 % at +20 pp; stage 4 ("≤ 1 failure of 40") rejects a neutral trait 60 % of the time at a 5 % flip rate under symmetric noise (26 % under mean-neutral noise). Expected admitted genes per seed are 0.0–1.3 of 10; P(arm C empty) ≈ 33–98 % across the modelled cells (**M**). (`EXP-0001-power-analysis.md` §0; C1–C3, R8.)
   - **H2 is under-powered at its own threshold and has no non-degeneracy clause.** The realized MDE on T_eval (n = 134 × 3 seeds) is ≈ 7–10 pp with independent seeds and ≈ 12–17 pp with redundant seeds, against the pre-registered +5 pp; the regression leg's power is 12–35 % at ρ = 1 (30–98 % at ρ = 0, but partly by construction because T_reg selects arm C). With |C| = 0, H2 is formally "supported" whenever B is significantly harmful, which supports "veto beats inherit-all", not causal selection. (C5, C6, V2, V3.)

## 7. What this work does not claim; residual uncertainty; what would change the conclusions

**Not claimed.** That EXP-0001 *will* fail (all harm statements are predictions under explicit models; no adapter, no G0 rate, no measured f or d0 exist). That any code at `6014bc7` is incorrect in its primary verdicts (the two major findings concern a secondary cell and evidence binding). That HeritAgent is "first" or "novel" in any component (the claim is the conjunction, pending cross-review). That the gate is "causal" in any sense beyond controlled interventions. Which product (Zcode / Codex) plays which role: the mapping is taken from the owner, not inferred. Anything about the human owner's decisions.

**Residual uncertainty (from the sources' own limits).** p0, f, the noise shape and the seed-sharing fraction ρ are unmeasured and move every power number by up to a factor of ≈ 2. The Pick2 minimum-plan derivation under the 8-step cap and the stage-2 allocation (4/2/4/3/3/4) are reviewer-computed (**U**). The accidental mining-pair rate depends on what the unbuilt adapter passes as `task`. H-level estimators are assumptions because the locked text is silent and no H-level analysis code exists. T1e, T5, T6 and joint H1/H2 behaviour were reproduced by rerun only, not re-derived. Line citations marked "PR" in the validity review are at `c6ce4c1` and may have shifted. The novelty adjudication is single-agent; several venues were unreachable; no sweep covers works after 2026-10-12, and the full texts of nine queued works are unread. The 3.10–3.13 test matrix was run at `0ce0a36` / `4ca5517`, not re-run at `6014bc7` (the failing file is unchanged there). The coordination relay-latency hypothesis and the PR #26 anomaly are unresolved. The subjective priors in the pre-registration draft are the author's, not estimates.

**What would change the conclusions.** A pilot G0 success rate ≥ 66.7 % on T_mine under a pre-registered step budget would remove the T_reg feasibility concern. A measured placebo flip rate f ≈ 0 with a deterministic backend would pin false-reject near the zero-noise value (≈ 85 % at +20 pp; the 2.5–5 % noise cells span ≈ 80–99.8 %, from P(promote) ≈ 0.002–0.2); if, in addition, mined traits deterministically rescue their source failure, false-reject at +20 pp falls to ≈ 67–71 % (**M**). An ADR adopting k > 1 stochastic lifetime rollouts removes the P0 finding at the cost of redefining "independent mining seeds" and "G0-solved". Adoption of an exact discordant-pair test would make stage 2 stricter, not looser. A cross-review that finds a work gating trait-level germline assimilation across agent generations on interventional evidence would flip the novelty position. CI results on 3.10–3.13 that differ from the scratch-copy validation would raise the portability severity.
